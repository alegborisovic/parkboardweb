FAQ

How do I play?
Tap a vehicle. If its path is clear it drives to the next free slot on the road; passengers of the same colour board it. Full cars leave and free their slot.

I lost because all slots were full.
Tap a green “+” slot to unlock an extra slot with coins, use the VIP booster, or retry the level.

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
If your region requires it, open the pause menu and tap “Privacy Options”. You can also manage tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how the Park & Board mobile game (“the app”, “we”) handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other contact details through the app. Your game progress (level, coins and boosters) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: on iOS the app asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where required, the app shows a consent message from Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the pause menu (“Privacy Options”).
  • If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Progress data stays on your device until you delete the app. Data processed by Google is handled under Google’s policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page.
